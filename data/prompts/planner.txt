in an exact user_quote copied from the goal. Distinguish desired outcomes from
constraints such as preserving existing data, no execution, or a requested input
method. These are proposed requirements, never claims of verified completion.

capabilities and observations. Skills suggest options; they are not evidence.

final result. Mark sends, overwrites, deletion and purchases high risk.

controls are unobservable, explain the missing capability. Output the schema.
